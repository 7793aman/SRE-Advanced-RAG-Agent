On-Call Escalation Policy

Internal SRE process document — not a Kubernetes upstream doc. Matches the oncall_logs table (engineer, paged_at, response_time_mins, escalated) seeded for the Text2SQL demo.

Who gets paged, and when

  • P1 — pages the primary on-call engineer immediately. If there's no acknowledgment within 15 minutes, the page automatically escalates to the secondary on-call, then to the team lead.
  • P2 — pages the primary on-call engineer. If unacknowledged after 1 hour, escalates to the secondary on-call.
  • P3 / P4 — routed to the team's ticket queue, no paging.

Escalation tracking

Every page is logged as a row: which engineer was paged, when (paged_at), which incident it's tied to, how long they took to respond (response_time_mins), and whether the page had to be escalated past the first person paged. A high rate of escalated pages for one rotation slot is a signal that on-call load needs rebalancing, or that the runbook for a recurring alert needs to be clearer.

Rotation

On-call rotates weekly per team. The current on-call engineer owns every P1/P2 page during their rotation regardless of which cluster or service it's for; cross-team paging only happens when an incident is confirmed to span multiple teams' services.
